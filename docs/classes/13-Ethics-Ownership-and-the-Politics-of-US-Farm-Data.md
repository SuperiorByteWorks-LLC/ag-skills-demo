## 📚 Content

### Farm Data Ownership and Intellectual Property

We explore who owns farm data and how contracts define usage rights.

Key points:

- Farmer ownership vs platform control
- Contract terms and data licensing
- Impacts on data portability

<details>
<summary><strong>💬 Speaker Notes</strong></summary>

- Provide one example of a common data ownership clause
- Encourage students to read terms before using platforms

</details>

### Privacy Concerns and Regulatory Landscape

We review privacy risks and emerging regulations affecting farm data.

Key points:

- Sensitive farm data types
- State-level privacy initiatives
- Consent and data sharing expectations

<details>
<summary><strong>💬 Speaker Notes</strong></summary>

- Compare farm data privacy to other industries
- Emphasize transparency and informed consent

</details>

### Algorithmic Bias and Fairness in Agriculture

We examine how models can disadvantage certain regions or farm sizes.

Key points:

- Biased training data and coverage gaps
- Equity impacts on small or underrepresented farms
- Mitigation strategies and audits

<details>
<summary><strong>💬 Speaker Notes</strong></summary>

- Encourage students to consider fairness in their final project
- Mention validation across multiple geographies

</details>

### Corporate Consolidation and Data Monopolies

We discuss market concentration and its impact on data access.

Key points:

- Platform lock-in and interoperability barriers
- Pricing power and data control
- Long-term impacts on innovation
